[ 03 / Approach ]

Three things I bring to a team.

/ 01

I look for the problem behind the problem.

Messy files aren't a designer problem if they're producing a customer problem. The work I'm proudest of usually starts with naming the second-order effect nobody else has connected yet.

/ 02

I design systems, not screens.

Twelve years in enterprise SaaS has taught me that one shared component beats fifty pixel-perfect comps. I build foundations other designers move faster on top of — with WCAG 2.2 baked in.

/ 03

I use AI as a multiplier, not a designer.

My workflow pairs structured Claude prompts with my own research and design intent. The model accelerates the work; the judgement stays mine. Loop and Guestchat are proof.
